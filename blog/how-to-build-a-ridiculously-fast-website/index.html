Does Speed Even Matter? Yes, It Does.

First off, why should you even care about how fast your website is?

You should definitely care, because a slow website will:

  • Make your conversion rates drop.
  • Make your visitors less satisfied.
  • Drop retention.
  • Lead to a lower ranking on Google.

Slow websites are especially a problem on slower devices with slower connections. On decent desktops with fast Internet, most sites feel okay. Even bad ones.

But if you target the slow end of the spectrum, you’ll see positive result across the board. On a budget phone with janky Internet, your site will still feel fast. Surprisingly fast. On a desktop with good Internet, it will feel unreal.
