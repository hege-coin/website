How To Deposit and Withdraw $HEGE From the Hege DAO

Learn how to deposit and withdraw $HEGE from the Hege DAO.

by Day

Info / October 21, 2025

This post is part of a series of posts about the Hege DAO, see How To Use the Hege DAO for an introduction and overview.

How to deposit and withdraw

NOTE: If you have never deposited into the Hege DAO before, please follow the instructions in How to Become a Member of the Hege DAO.

If you have made the initial deposit into the Hege DAO, follow the instructions below to deposit more $HEGE, or to withdraw some of your deposited tokens.

How to deposit $HEGE into the Hege DAO

  1. Go to the Hege DAO page on Realms. It should look something like this. Click “Connect Wallet” in the upper right corner of the page to connect your wallet.
  1. If necessary, select your Solana wallet to complete the connection.
  1. When you have connected your wallet, you can see how many $HEGE tokens you have deposited in the upper right corner, right next to the banner.
  1. Click “Deposit”.
  1. Select how many $HEGE you want to deposit.
  1. Then click “Deposit”.
  1. Confirm the transaction in your Solana wallet.
  1. That’s it! When the transaction has confirmed on the Solana blockchain, the page will update to show your new balance.

How to withdraw $HEGE from the Hege DAO

  1. Go to the Hege DAO page on Realms. It should look something like this. Click “Connect Wallet” in the upper right corner of the page to connect your wallet.
  1. If necessary, select your Solana wallet to complete the connection.
  1. When you have connected your wallet, you can see how many $HEGE tokens you have deposited in the upper right corner, right next to the banner.
  1. Click “Withdraw”.
  1. Select how many $HEGE you want to withdraw.
  1. Then click “Withdraw”.
  1. Confirm the transaction in your Solana wallet.
  1. That’s it! When the transaction has confirmed on the Solana blockchain, the page will update to show your new balance.
